`NodeResizer`, `NodeResizeControl`, and `NodeToolbar` are now exported directly from `@xyflow/vue`, and the resize-control styles ship with `@xyflow/vue/dist/style.css`. The `@vue-flow/node-resizer` and `@vue-flow/node-toolbar` packages are deprecated; install only `@xyflow/vue` going forward.
