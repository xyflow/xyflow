`HandleElement` is removed in favor of `HandleBounds`, and `EdgePositions` in favor of `EdgePosition`. The `<Handle>` props `connectableStart`/`connectableEnd` are renamed to `isConnectableStart`/`isConnectableEnd`, `OnConnectStartParams` fields are now nullable, and the deprecated `Project` type is no longer re-exported.
