`HandleElement` is removed in favor of `Handle`, and `EdgePositions` in favor of `EdgePosition`. `<Handle>`'s `connectableStart`/`connectableEnd` props are renamed to `isConnectableStart`/`isConnectableEnd`, `OnConnectStartParams` fields are now nullable, and the deprecated `Project` type is no longer re-exported.
